## Who we are

Beco Interiors is a Kenyan interior solutions company focused on bringing together innovative
materials, functional solutions and contemporary design for residential and commercial spaces.

We believe that great interiors are not simply about how a space looks. They are about how it
feels, how it functions and how well every element works together. This is why we are committed
to providing quality products, practical solutions and a seamless client experience from the
first conversation to project completion.

As a growing company in Kenya's interior and building materials industry, Beco is building a
reputation around quality, reliability, innovation and attention to detail. Our goal is simple:
to make it easier for homeowners, interior designers, contractors and developers to access
well curated interior solutions from one trusted partner.

## What we do

Our product offering includes premium sintered stone, wall panels, kitchen accessories,
cabinet handles, SPC flooring and selected furniture accessories.

Our sintered stone range is available in both **12mm and 15mm** formats, giving clients and
designers greater flexibility depending on the application and desired finish. Beyond supplying
materials, we support clients through **fabrication and installation** of sintered stone,
helping ensure that the final result reflects the quality of the material itself.

We are particularly passionate about materials that combine aesthetics with performance:
solutions that look beautiful while also being practical, durable and suited to modern living.

## What we stand for

At Beco, we believe in quality without compromise, thoughtful design, honest communication and
service that goes beyond the sale.

We understand that choosing materials for a project can be overwhelming. There are
specifications to consider, budgets to work within, finishes to compare and installation
details to get right. Our role is to simplify that process.

Rather than simply selling products, we aim to understand what the client is trying to achieve
and then guide them towards solutions that make sense for their space, lifestyle and project
requirements. We believe in selling an experience, not just a product.

## Our team

Behind Beco is a growing team with different responsibilities but one shared objective:
delivering a smooth and professional client experience.

- **Leadership and management** oversee strategic direction, operations, marketing, sales and
  customer experience.
- **The sales team** works directly with clients, designers, contractors and other industry
  professionals to understand project requirements and recommend suitable solutions.
- **Field sales agents** extend this reach beyond the showroom. They engage with potential
  clients, designers, contractors and businesses in the field, introduce Beco's solutions,
  identify opportunities and build relationships that grow the network.
- **Technical and installation teams** bring approved solutions to life, with careful attention
  to measurements, fabrication, finishing and installation.

Every role matters because the client experience is a team effort.

## The client journey

1. **Discovery.** Every project starts with a conversation. A client may find Beco through
   social media, a referral, a designer, a contractor, a field agent, or by visiting the
   showroom. Beco first seeks to understand the project: what is being built or renovated, the
   intended application, the preferred aesthetic and other requirements.
2. **Consultation and selection.** Beco guides the client through the options: viewing samples,
   comparing colours, textures, finishes, thicknesses and applications, and discussing the
   practical considerations of different materials. The objective is an informed decision that
   suits the project, not the most expensive option.
3. **Measurement and assessment.** For solutions requiring fabrication and installation,
   accurate measurements and project details are essential. Site visits and assessments,
   where applicable, establish the real requirements before a quotation is prepared.
4. **Quotation.** The sales team prepares a quotation outlining the selected materials,
   quantities, fabrication, installation and other applicable costs, clear and easy to
   understand so the client knows exactly what they are considering.
5. **Decision and closing.** Once the client is comfortable, the sales team guides them through
   confirmation of selections, payment requirements, production timelines and installation
   arrangements. Closing a sale is the beginning of delivering on the promise, not the end of
   the relationship.
6. **Production, delivery and installation.** The relevant teams coordinate production,
   preparation, delivery and installation. For sintered stone and wall panels that Beco
   installs, the team works to ensure the finished result meets the agreed specification and
   the quality expected of the brand.
7. **Completion and client experience.** A project should end with a client who feels they
   were listened to, properly guided and well taken care of throughout.
