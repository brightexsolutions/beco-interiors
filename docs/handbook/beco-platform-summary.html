Beco sells through quotations, so the whole platform is built around one moment: a salesperson raising, pricing and sending a branded quote from a phone faster than writing it on paper.

01
The big picture
What you have now

Two places, one set of records. Customers use the website. Your team uses the dashboard. Both read and write the same secure records, so a request made on the website is waiting in the dashboard a moment later.

For customers

Browse and ask

See every range and product, add items to a quote list, and send it. They get a reference and a WhatsApp link straight away.

For sales

Quote and close

Claim requests, price them, send a branded PDF by email or WhatsApp, and turn a yes into an order and a receipt.

For managers

See and decide

Approve special prices, follow every quote by stage, read the month's figures, and control who can see what.

The records

A secure database

Products, quotes, orders, customers and settings live in one database on Beco's own account. It refuses anything a person is not allowed to do, whatever screen they use.

The photographs

Stored once, shown fast

Every photograph is saved in three sizes, so a phone downloads a small one and a laptop a sharp one. Storage is on Beco's own Cloudflare account.

The messages

Email and WhatsApp

Quotes and receipts go out from quotes@beco.co.ke, or as a PDF in a WhatsApp chat with the number already filled in.

The hosting

Always on, quick everywhere

Both sites are served from a global network, so pages load quickly in Nairobi and abroad, with a copy for testing changes before they reach you.

On a phone
Quoting at the counter

The dashboard is built to be used standing at the counter with one hand. The main screens sit in a bar at the bottom of the phone, with New quote in the middle.

1
Pick the customer.Search by name or phone. A number already on file brings up the existing customer, so nobody is entered twice.
2
Add the products.Tick several from the catalogue at once. A product that comes in sizes lists one row per size, each with its own price. Anything not listed can be added as a custom item.
3
Price and send.Adjust quantities and prices, then send the branded PDF by email or WhatsApp, or print it.

Extra access, without a new role

A person can be let into a section beyond their role, for example a product manager who should see quotes. Granted sections open read only: they can look, not change, and the screen says View only. Brightex can grant any section; a Beco manager given staff management can grant what they hold themselves.

08
Help built in
Finding your way
  • Search anything. The search box, or Command K, jumps to any screen or finds a quote by name, phone or reference.
  • Never left wondering. Buttons show they are working, a bar shows the next screen loading, and a dark bar says when the connection drops and nothing will save.
  • Mistakes are hard to make. Anything that cannot be undone, such as marking a quote lost or deleting a product, asks first and says exactly what will happen.
09
The reasoning
Why it is built this way
Quotes, not a shopping cart

Beco's customers buy several materials for a project and agree a price, so the website collects a request and your team prices it. Nobody pays online, so there is no card handling and nothing to go wrong at checkout.

WhatsApp as the second door

Many of your customers prefer to talk on WhatsApp. Every page offers it after the quote request, and the PDF can be sent in a chat with the number filled in.

Any price, with one person checking

A salesperson can agree a price on the spot so a deal is never lost waiting, but a special price must be approved by a manager before the quote is final.

Every change recorded

Who changed what, and when, is kept for prices, quotes, orders, customers and settings. Records with commercial meaning are archived rather than erased, so the history always points to something real.

A practice copy, separate from the real one

Changes are tried on a separate copy filled with made up customers before they reach you. Your real customers' names and numbers are never used for testing.

Photographs on Cloudflare

Showing photographs costs nothing extra however many people visit, so a busy month never brings a surprise bill. Cost grows only with the size of the catalogue.

Read only access instead of new roles

Letting one person see a section is a tick box, and it can never let them change records their role does not allow.

Beco owns the accounts

Your domain, records, photographs, email and analytics are on Beco's own accounts. If Brightex stopped working with Beco tomorrow, everything would keep running.

10
Protection
Keeping it safe
Sign in

Staff accounts only

Nobody can sign themselves up. Each person gets an account and must set their own password on first sign in. A deactivated account is locked out at once.

Permissions

Checked twice

Every screen checks who you are, and the records themselves refuse anything your role does not allow. Hiding a button is never the only protection.

Testing

Over 3,300 automated checks

Every change is tested before it goes live, including that each kind of user is refused what they should not do.

Backups

Saved before every change

A full copy of the records is taken before each update to the database. An automatic nightly backup is planned and listed in section 12.
