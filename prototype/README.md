# Approved prototype

The static build the client reviewed and approved. **Preserved as the structural and
behavioural reference, not as code to extend.**

## What carries forward

The 8px spacing base, the section rhythm, the eyebrow pattern, the type scale relationships,
the mega menu structure, the scroll reveal in `beco-pages.js`, the product data shape in
`beco-shop.html`, and the quote wizard's three step flow.

## What does not

**The palette.** This was drawn before anyone had seen the brand guideline, so the warm cream
`#FAF7F5`, brick red `#C8281E`, linen `#E6DDD4` and gold `#C4972A` are retired rather than
blended. See D2.

**The fonts.** Three families loaded render blocking from Google Fonts. Replaced by self hosted
subset Titillium Web and Cormorant Garamond.

**The hero.** Centered text over a darkened full bleed photograph is the pattern the brief
explicitly rules out. Replaced by the pinned split scroll, D30.

**The footer.** Currently grey link columns and a copyright line, which is exactly what
Section 11 forbids. Rebuilt as a genuine closing section carrying the three conversion actions.

**The type sizes.** 11px and 9.5px labels are below the 16px floor.

**The catalog.** Lighting, and the 520+ product claim, do not match the real Drive taxonomy.

`beco-design-system.html` stays useful: it is the reference the M3 component gallery is held
against, side by side.
